✦ Guild Locations ✦

The Adventurer’s Guild maintains a connected network of headquarters, regional branches, minor offices, outposts, and specialized facilities throughout Avadon. These locations allow Guild members to find contracts, report completed assignments, receive training, conduct research, obtain supplies, and secure lodging while traveling.

The size and services of each location depend on the needs of the surrounding region. Major cities may support full Guild halls with archives, training areas, and permanent staff, while villages and frontier settlements may rely on smaller contract houses or roadside posts.

The Guild's Home World

The World of Avadon

Avadon is a vast and varied world divided among three great continents: Avadur in the west, Eryndor at its center, and Mythoria in the east. Across these lands are sixty-eight recognized countries, each shaped by its own cultures, governments, religions, magical traditions, and conflicts. Between them lie great oceans, island kingdoms, inland seas, mountain ranges, forests, deserts, and territories that remain only partially explored.

Long ago, a star fell during a battle over a mineral-rich border. Its impact scarred the land, shattered the object, and released magic across the world. That magic now moves through Avadon along ley lines, waterways, mountain chains, geological boundaries, and places of spiritual importance. Some regions have embraced its power, others regulate or fear it, and a few actively suppress it.

Beyond the protection of settled lands, Avadon remains filled with forgotten ruins, dangerous wilderness, magical disturbances, unusual creatures, and evidence of civilizations older than recorded history. Political rivalries, shifting borders, hazardous roads, and supernatural threats can turn even an ordinary journey into an expedition.

The Adventurer’s Guild was established to connect the people of Avadon with those capable of facing these dangers. Its network of headquarters, regional branches, contract houses, outposts, and specialized facilities provides adventurers with contracts, training, supplies, research, lodging, and reliable support wherever their travels may lead.

Headquarters

Grand Guild Hall

The Grand Guild Hall is the public center of the Adventurer’s Guild and the primary gathering place for members visiting headquarters. Its main chamber contains announcement boards, reception desks, meeting areas, and spaces where adventurers can speak with Guild officials.

Primary services: Member registration, general contracts, official announcements, meetings, ceremonies, and public Guild business.

Headquarters

Council Chambers

The Council Chambers are used by senior Guild leaders to review policy, discuss regional threats, approve major expenditures, and coordinate operations involving multiple branches. Representatives from allied organizations may also be invited to attend important meetings.

Primary services: Guild leadership meetings, strategic planning, policy decisions, emergency coordination, and diplomatic discussions.

Headquarters

Master's Office

The Master’s Office serves as the private workplace of the Guild Master. Important reports, confidential assignments, disciplinary matters, and requests requiring the highest level of Guild approval are brought here.

Primary services: Executive decisions, private meetings, confidential contracts, senior appointments, and final Guild authorization.

Headquarters

Hall of Records

The Hall of Records preserves the official history of the Guild. Its clerks maintain membership files, completed contract reports, disciplinary records, financial ledgers, regional maps, and accounts of important discoveries.

Primary services: Member records, contract histories, official reports, maps, legal documents, and historical research.

Headquarters

High Contract Office

The High Contract Office reviews assignments involving powerful enemies, political consequences, restricted information, or threats extending beyond a single region. Access is generally limited to experienced adventurers and authorized Guild staff.

Primary services: High-rank contracts, classified assignments, threat assessment, mission authorization, and specialized operational support.

Major Branch

Aeridell Branch Hall

The Aeridell Branch Hall coordinates Guild activity throughout Aeridell and its surrounding communities. The branch is known for assignments involving wilderness travel, ancient sites, regional diplomacy, and threats affecting settlements beyond the protection of city walls.

Primary services: Regional contracts, wilderness maps, local guides, diplomatic contacts, member lodging, and field supplies.

Major Branch

Park Royal Hall

Park Royal Hall serves as the Guild’s principal branch within the Kingdom of Park. Its members regularly work with royal officials, city authorities, merchants, and magical organizations on politically sensitive or high-profile assignments.

Primary services: Royal contracts, political liaison work, magical investigations, city assignments, secure meetings, and regional coordination.

Major Branch

Rivergate Guild Hall

Rivergate Guild Hall oversees contracts connected to waterways, trade routes, merchant caravans, docks, and river communities. Its adventurers frequently protect valuable cargo, investigate missing shipments, and respond to threats disrupting regional commerce.

Primary services: Trade-route contracts, caravan coordination, shipping records, merchant contacts, river maps, and cargo protection.

Major Branch

Highwatch Guild Hall

Highwatch Guild Hall supports adventurers operating in remote, elevated, and difficult terrain. The branch monitors isolated roads, mountain settlements, abandoned ruins, and dangerous regions where help may be several days away.

Primary services: Mountain contracts, survival equipment, remote-area reports, rescue coordination, guides, and secure lodging.

Minor Branch

Frontier Outposts

Frontier outposts are small, fortified Guild stations positioned near dangerous borders, remote roads, and unsettled regions. They provide adventurers with a safe place to report threats, replenish basic supplies, and request emergency assistance.

Primary services: Local contracts, threat reports, emergency shelter, basic supplies, message delivery, and frontier maps.

Minor Branch

Village Contract Houses

Village contract houses provide smaller communities with direct access to the Adventurer’s Guild. These locations are often operated by a single Guild clerk, retired adventurer, or local representative who records requests and posts approved assignments.

Primary services: Local notices, basic contracts, member check-in, community reports, and communication with larger Guild branches.

Minor Branch

Roadside Guild Posts

Roadside Guild posts are simple facilities established along heavily traveled routes. They offer a recognizable and secure stopping point where Guild members can exchange messages, review road conditions, and rest during long journeys.

Primary services: Travel notices, message exchange, temporary shelter, road maps, water, and limited emergency supplies.

Minor Branch

Regional Guild Offices

Regional Guild offices handle administrative work in areas that do not require a complete Guild hall. Their staff coordinate contracts, maintain local records, communicate with nearby branches, and support adventurers working throughout the region.

Primary services: Contract administration, regional records, member verification, scheduling, correspondence, and branch coordination.

Special Facility

Training Grounds

Guild training grounds provide controlled spaces where members can practice combat, survival techniques, spellcasting, teamwork, and specialized professional skills under the supervision of qualified instructors.

Primary services: Skill training, combat practice, Field Training and Observation, equipment testing, and team exercises.

Special Facility

Guild Archives

Guild archives contain historical reports, creature records, maps, magical research, expedition journals, and information gathered during completed contracts. Access to sensitive sections depends on a member’s rank and authorization.

Primary services: Research, maps, historical records, threat information, expedition reports, and restricted documents.

Special Facility

Supply Depots

Supply depots store equipment and materials commonly needed during Guild operations. Available resources vary by location, but most depots maintain basic tools, travel supplies, medical materials, replacement gear, and emergency provisions.

Primary services: Equipment distribution, replacement supplies, emergency provisions, repair materials, and mission-specific resources.

Special Facility

Member Lodging

Member lodging provides Guild adventurers with safe and reliable accommodations while traveling on official business. The quality and privacy of the rooms may depend on the facility, the member’s rank, and current availability.

Primary services: Sleeping quarters, common rooms, meals, secure storage, bathing facilities, and extended recovery stays.

Special Facility

Guild Stables

Guild stables care for horses, pack animals, mounts, and other approved forms of transportation used by adventurers. Stable staff may also arrange replacement mounts or prepare animals for long-distance assignments.

Primary services: Animal care, feed, secure stalls, mount rental, transportation preparation, and basic veterinary assistance.

Special Facility

Research Laboratories

Guild research laboratories allow approved specialists to examine magical objects, unusual creatures, alchemical materials, curses, diseases, and evidence recovered during difficult contracts.

Primary services: Magical analysis, alchemical testing, artifact examination, specimen research, identification, and controlled experimentation.
