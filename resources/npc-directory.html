✦ NPC Directory ✦

This directory records notable guild officers, scholars, merchants, specialists, and trusted contacts encountered throughout Avadon. Entries contain information commonly available to guild members and avoid confidential reports, hidden loyalties, and other restricted records.

✦ Guild Officers ✦

OM

Administration & Logistics

Oren Mossledger

Position
Quartermaster-General
Office
Stores and Provisioning
Specialty
Contracts, records, finances, permits, and supplies

Oren believes heroic work can only endure when it is supported by dependable records, lawful agreements, and carefully managed resources. He oversees much of the Guild's administrative and logistical work.

“Heroism needs structure to survive.”

SB

Field Operations

Sarya Briarwatch

Position
Field Captain and Training Master
Affiliation
Former Thornroot Warden and Hunter's Accord captain
Specialty
Recruit training, dangerous assignments, and field safety

Sarya trains young hunters and adventurers to treat dangerous work as a responsibility rather than a test of pride. She expects clear orders, proper preparation, and fair compensation for those sent into the field.

“A Guild is responsible for everyone it sends out.”

TR

Construction & Facilities

Thalara Rootwright

Profession
Livingwood architect
Affiliation
Sylvan Foresters' Compact
Specialty
Guildhall design, expansion, surveys, and local approvals

Thalara designs structures that belong to their surroundings rather than simply occupying them. She can survey prospective sites, plan a guildhall, and help navigate the environmental and civic requirements of Sylvanthorn.

“A Guild must belong to its surroundings.”

✦ Scholars & Records ✦

OM

History & Archives

Othriel Mossward

Position
Senior Archivist
Location
The Root Archives, Sylvanthorn
Specialty
Historical routes, surviving structures, names, maps, and old records

Othriel is an elderly elf known for his remarkable memory and robes filled with more pockets than seems possible. He cares for records written in ink, carved into stone, and grown directly into preserved wood.

“A memorable request is still a record.”

✦ Merchants & Specialists ✦

TM

Gems & Crystals

Talen Mire

Species
Gnome
Business
Prism & Cut
Location
Enthalor

Talen sells gems, crystals, and crafted jewelry from a quiet shop filled with precise tools. He creates custom pieces, identifies unusual stones, and studies the properties held within rare crystals.

“You don't shape the stone. You uncover it.”

EV

Clothing & Textiles

Elira Voss

Species
Human
Business
Sunweave Silks
Location
Enthalor

Elira provides fine clothing, textiles, and custom garments for travelers who need to make the right impression. Her work is valued by diplomats, performers, merchants, and adventurers preparing for formal occasions.

RT

Travel & Survival

Renn Tal

Species
Half-Orc
Business
Trailwright Outfitters
Location
Enthalor

Renn supplies adventuring gear, travel equipment, and practical survival tools. Customers value advice that comes from experience rather than salesmanship, especially when preparing for difficult roads.

VS

Magic & Enchantment

Vaelor Syn

Species
Elf
Business
The Runed Exchange
Location
Kingdom of Park

Vaelor deals in magical items, scrolls, enchantments, and the identification of unusual magical effects. Guild members are advised to bring clear records of an object's origin before requesting a full appraisal.

✦ Travelers & Contacts ✦

EH

Caravan & Trade Roads

Edrik Haleven

Profession
Caravan driver and trade-road contact
Common Route
North toward the Kingdom of Park
Specialty
Goods transport, wagon travel, and long-distance routes

Edrik is an experienced caravan driver who regularly seeks capable guards for difficult deliveries. He is practical, observant, and familiar with the risks found along the roads between major settlements.
