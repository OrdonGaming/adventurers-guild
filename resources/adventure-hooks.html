✦ Adventure Hooks ✦

Adventure hooks are short story seeds that give Game Masters a starting situation, a question that demands an answer, and enough uncertainty for the players to shape what happens next. Each hook below can be used as a brief side quest, expanded into a full session, or connected to a larger campaign arc.

Building an Adventure Hook

1

Begin with Change

Something has recently happened that interrupts normal life: a person disappears, a road closes, a creature behaves strangely, or an old object becomes active.

2

Create a Question

Give the players something they will want to understand. Who caused the problem? Why now? What is being concealed? What happens if no one intervenes?

3

Add a Complication

Introduce a deadline, disagreement, false assumption, dangerous environment, competing group, or consequence that prevents an easy solution.

4

Leave Room for Choice

Avoid deciding the ending in advance. Present people, evidence, and pressures that allow the players to decide how the situation should be resolved.

✦ Settlements & Communities ✦

Community Mystery

The Bell That Refuses to Ring

A settlement's warning bell has gone silent even though the bell, rope, and tower appear undamaged.

Question
What is preventing the bell from sounding?
Complication
The bell rings normally when removed from the tower, but no resident will explain what happened there years ago.
Escalation
Warning signs appear along the road, and the settlement may soon need the bell more than ever.
Suggested Rank
Bronze or Silver

Social Investigation

The Empty Morning Market

Every merchant arrives for market day, but no customers appear and no one can remember deciding to stay home.

Question
Why are residents avoiding the market without realizing it?
Complication
One vendor is unaffected and is earning more coin than ever.
Escalation
The same behavior begins spreading to other public places.
Suggested Rank
Silver

Identity & Records

Names Missing from the Ledger

Several residents discover that their names have vanished from tax records, property deeds, and family histories.

Question
Who benefits when a person no longer officially exists?
Complication
The affected residents begin forgetting one another.
Escalation
A Guild member's name disappears next.
Suggested Rank
Gold

✦ Wilderness & Exploration ✦

Navigation

The Trail That Moves at Dusk

Travelers report that a familiar forest trail changes direction each evening and returns them to where they began.

Question
Is the forest protecting something, trapping travelers, or asking for help?
Complication
The path only remains stable when followed by someone who has never used it before.
Escalation
A supply caravan becomes trapped with medicine needed by a nearby village.
Suggested Rank
Bronze or Silver

Natural Disturbance

The Stone Beneath the River

A drought exposes a carved stone beneath a riverbed, and nearby animals begin gathering around it after sunset.

Question
What was the stone meant to mark, contain, or remember?
Complication
The animals peacefully prevent anyone from approaching it.
Escalation
Cracks appear in the stone as the river continues to fall.
Suggested Rank
Silver or Gold

Lost Expedition

The Camp That Returned Without Its Travelers

Tents, packs, and a cold cooking fire appear overnight beside a road, although the expedition using them vanished months ago.

Question
Did the camp return from somewhere, or is it waiting for the travelers to come back?
Complication
Each item is clean, maintained, and slightly newer than when the expedition departed.
Escalation
A journal begins recording new entries during the night.
Suggested Rank
Gold

✦ Mystery & Magic ✦

Arcane Anomaly

The Spell That Cast Itself Again

A harmless spell performed in a training hall repeats itself every midnight without a caster present.

Question
What is remembering the spell, and why is the repetition becoming stronger?
Complication
Each repetition includes a detail that was not part of the original casting.
Escalation
The next predicted variation could damage the entire hall.
Suggested Rank
Silver

Dreams & Prophecy

A Dream Shared by Strangers

People from different regions describe the same unfamiliar room, the same locked door, and the same voice asking for aid.

Question
Where is the room, and who is reaching through the dreams?
Complication
Each dreamer remembers a different key.
Escalation
One dreamer fails to wake.
Suggested Rank
Gold or Platinum

Enchanted Object

The Mirror That Shows Honest Intentions

A merchant acquires a mirror that does not reflect faces; it shows what each viewer intends to do next.

Question
Is the mirror revealing intentions, creating them, or choosing what people believe?
Complication
Powerful officials want the mirror used during negotiations and trials.
Escalation
The mirror shows an assassination no one admits planning.
Suggested Rank
Gold

✦ Guild & Factions ✦

Guild Administration

Two Contracts, One Objective

Two Guild offices issue separate contracts concerning the same location, but the requested outcomes directly conflict.

Question
Is the conflict a mistake, a political struggle, or a test of the Guild's authority?
Complication
Both contracts carry valid seals and were approved by respected officers.
Escalation
Another adventuring party accepts the opposing contract.
Suggested Rank
Silver or Gold

Internal Security

The Missing Guild Seal

An official seal used to authorize contracts disappears, and false assignments begin appearing on distant quest boards.

Question
Who is issuing the false contracts, and what are they trying to accomplish?
Complication
Some fraudulent contracts are helping communities the Guild overlooked.
Escalation
A false assignment sends inexperienced adventurers into genuine danger.
Suggested Rank
Gold

Legacy & Reputation

The Retired Party's Final Request

A famous adventuring party asks the Guild to finish one contract they deliberately abandoned decades ago.

Question
Why did celebrated heroes refuse to complete the quest?
Complication
Each retired member tells a different version of what happened.
Escalation
The unfinished contract begins affecting the present.
Suggested Rank
Platinum or Adamantine

Expanding a Hook into a Quest

Add Three Clues

Prepare at least three different ways the players can learn important information. Clues may come from people, locations, objects, records, magic, or observation.

Add Two Complications

Choose one complication that is visible at the beginning and another that appears after the party commits to a course of action.

Add One Meaningful Choice

Give the party a decision that affects people, resources, relationships, or future events rather than changing only the order of encounters.

Define the Consequences

Decide what changes after complete success, partial success, failure, delay, or refusal. These consequences help the world respond naturally to player decisions.

Turn a Hook into a Contract

Select a story seed, decide what the Guild knows, and use the Quest Templates page to develop the objective, classification, reward, restrictions, and Game Master preparation notes.
