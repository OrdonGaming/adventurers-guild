✦ Quest Templates ✦

These templates help Game Masters create clear, consistent contracts for The Adventurer's Guild. Each format separates the information posted for adventurers from the private notes a Game Master may use to prepare clues, complications, consequences, and alternate outcomes.

Using the Templates

Player-Facing Information

Include only details the Guild would reasonably place on a public contract: the objective, location, known dangers, restrictions, required rank, deadline, and offered reward.

Game Master Notes

Record the truth behind the contract, likely clues, important NPCs, complications, time pressure, possible consequences, and conditions for partial or complete success.

Adaptation

Replace the bracketed prompts with details from the current region. A single template can support a brief side quest, a full session, or the beginning of a longer story arc.

Game Master Preparation

  • What is actually happening?
  • Why was the contract posted?
  • Who benefits from the party's success or failure?
  • What information is incomplete, mistaken, or concealed?
  • Which clues lead toward the solution?
  • What complication changes the situation?
  • What happens if the party delays or abandons the quest?
  • What counts as complete, partial, or failed resolution?

✦ Quest Classification Guide ✦

Suggested quest scope by Guild rank
Rank Typical Scope Common Risks Recommended Use
Bronze Local and clearly defined Limited danger with available support Training, simple investigation, delivery, or minor assistance
Silver Regional or multi-step Moderate danger and incomplete information Escort, rescue, recovery, or wilderness travel
Gold Major settlement or regional threat Serious opposition and difficult choices Complex investigations, dangerous creatures, or faction conflict
Platinum Kingdom-wide or strategically important Severe danger with lasting consequences Critical expeditions, major disasters, or powerful enemies
Adamantine Realm-shaping or legendary Extreme danger and irreversible consequences Ancient threats, national crises, or world-changing discoveries

[Time, location, witness, and observable evidence]
Requested Outcome
[Verified report, recovered evidence, identified cause, or intervention]
Restrictions
[Preserve evidence, avoid public alarm, do not confront suspects, or protect privacy]

Game Master Prompts

  • What is the true cause?
  • What three clues can reveal it?
  • Which witness is mistaken or withholding information?
  • What becomes worse if the party follows the wrong lead?
  • What noncombat solution is available?

Game Master Prompts

  • Why is the escort or cargo important?
  • Who wants to delay, steal, redirect, or destroy it?

  • What difficult choice could change the route?
