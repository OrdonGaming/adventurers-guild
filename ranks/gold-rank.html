Guild Classification

Gold Rank

Character Levels 9–12

Gold adventurers are respected specialists and field leaders. Their contracts often protect major settlements, important officials, valuable resources, or the stability of an entire region.

✦ What Gold Rank Means ✦

Gold Rank represents proven expertise. Members are trusted to lead mixed-rank teams, make decisions without direct supervision, and represent the Guild when dealing with powerful organizations.

✦ Gold Rank Stages ✦

Gold 1

Character Level 9

  • Reach character level 9 and complete the advancement requirements following the previous major rank.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +9 guild-training bonus.
  • Gain access to Gold contracts and major regional assignments.
  • Receive private or semi-private Guild quarters when available.

Gold 2

Character Level 10

  • Reach character level 10 and complete the advancement requirements following Gold 1.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +10 guild-training bonus.
  • Receive a 15% Guild merchant discount.
  • Use restricted archives concerning known regional threats.

Gold 3

Character Level 11

  • Reach character level 11 and complete the advancement requirements following Gold 2.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +11 guild-training bonus.
  • Receive priority Guild healing and recovery support.
  • Lead mixed-rank teams and recommend lower-rank promotions.

Gold 4

Character Level 12

  • Reach character level 12 and complete the advancement requirements following Gold 3.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +12 guild-training bonus.
  • Request an approved uncommon magic item loan.
  • Qualify for Platinum 1 after reaching level 13.

Guild Benefit

Skill Training

Each incremental promotion allows the character to train one chosen skill with an appropriate Guild NPC. The training grants an additional bonus on top of the character’s existing skill modifier.

The maximum guild-training bonus equals the character level represented by the current rank stage. Gold 1 permits a +9 bonus, while Gold 4 permits a +12 bonus.

Training the same skill again replaces its previous guild-training bonus with the new higher value; earlier Guild bonuses to that same skill do not stack together.

✦ Benefits and Privileges ✦

Major Contracts

Accept assignments affecting cities and regions.

Leadership Authority

Lead mixed-rank teams and coordinate Guild operations.

Merchant Discount

Receive a 15% discount from participating Guild merchants.

Restricted Research

Use selected archives concerning dangerous creatures and artifacts.

Priority Recovery

Receive priority access to Guild healers during active operations.

Magic Item Loans

Gold 4 members may request an approved uncommon magic item loan.

✦ Typical Gold Contracts ✦

Gold contracts may involve major monsters, coordinated enemies, political disputes, restricted locations, dangerous artifacts, or threats requiring specialized knowledge.

Advancement

Characters should normally complete three to five successful quests, earn the required advancement experience or milestone, and reach the minimum character level for the next stage. Incremental promotions may provide currency or recognition, while advancement into a new major rank should include a Rank Advancement Ceremony and a level-appropriate magic item.

Dungeon Master Guidance

Gold quests should make the party’s decisions matter beyond one contract. Use political consequences, public reputation, and difficult tradeoffs.

Guild-training bonuses are intentionally powerful and are added to the character’s existing skill modifier. Treat them as a high-powered homebrew reward. Upgrade rather than repeatedly stack the Guild bonus for the same skill.

The reward table is a campaign guideline. Adjust it for campaign economy, party resources, mission duration, and consequences. All rewards are listed per individual character.
