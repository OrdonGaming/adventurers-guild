Guild Classification

Platinum Rank

Character Levels 13–16

Platinum adventurers are elite Guild operatives entrusted with international threats, powerful enemies, and missions whose failure could alter the future of kingdoms.

✦ What Platinum Rank Means ✦

Platinum Rank signifies exceptional ability and extraordinary trust. Members are expected to command expeditions, advise Guild leadership, and act with Guild authority in dangerous or politically sensitive situations.

✦ Platinum Rank Stages ✦

Platinum 1

Character Level 13

  • Reach character level 13 and complete the advancement requirements following the previous major rank.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +13 guild-training bonus.
  • Gain access to Platinum contracts and international assignments.
  • Receive secure lodging and protected meeting space.

Platinum 2

Character Level 14

  • Reach character level 14 and complete the advancement requirements following Platinum 1.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +14 guild-training bonus.
  • Receive a 20% Guild merchant discount.
  • Use advanced archives and approved sealed threat reports.

Platinum 3

Character Level 15

  • Reach character level 15 and complete the advancement requirements following Platinum 2.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +15 guild-training bonus.
  • Request secure transport, specialists, or diplomatic introductions.
  • Command large expeditions and multiple adventuring teams.

Platinum 4

Character Level 16

  • Reach character level 16 and complete the advancement requirements following Platinum 3.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +16 guild-training bonus.
  • Request an approved rare magic item loan.
  • Qualify for Adamantine 1 after reaching level 17.

Guild Benefit

Skill Training

Each incremental promotion allows the character to train one chosen skill with an appropriate Guild NPC. The training grants an additional bonus on top of the character’s existing skill modifier.

The maximum guild-training bonus equals the character level represented by the current rank stage. Platinum 1 permits a +13 bonus, while Platinum 4 permits a +16 bonus.

Training the same skill again replaces its previous guild-training bonus with the new higher value; earlier Guild bonuses to that same skill do not stack together.

✦ Benefits and Privileges ✦

International Contracts

Accept assignments involving kingdoms, planes, or major powers.

Expedition Command

Command large Guild operations and several adventuring teams.

Merchant Discount

Receive a 20% discount from participating Guild merchants.

Advanced Archives

Use selected sealed records and strategic threat assessments.

Operational Support

Request secure travel, specialists, or diplomatic introductions.

Rare Item Loans

Platinum 4 members may request an approved rare magic item loan.

✦ Typical Platinum Contracts ✦

Platinum contracts may involve planar disturbances, legendary monsters, hostile nations, powerful magical organizations, or crises requiring secrecy and substantial resources.

Advancement

Characters should normally complete three to five successful quests, earn the required advancement experience or milestone, and reach the minimum character level for the next stage. Incremental promotions may provide currency or recognition, while advancement into a new major rank should include a Rank Advancement Ceremony and a level-appropriate magic item.

Dungeon Master Guidance

Platinum quests should feel exceptional and consequential. Give meaningful resources, then make planning, alliances, and judgment matter as much as combat power.

Guild-training bonuses are intentionally powerful and are added to the character’s existing skill modifier. Treat them as a high-powered homebrew reward. Upgrade rather than repeatedly stack the Guild bonus for the same skill.

The reward table is a campaign guideline. Adjust it for campaign economy, party resources, mission duration, and consequences. All rewards are listed per individual character.
