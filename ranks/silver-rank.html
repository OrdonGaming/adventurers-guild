Guild Classification

Silver Rank

Character Levels 5–8

Silver adventurers are proven Guild members trusted with regional travel, organized threats, and assignments that demand stronger judgment and greater independence.

✦ What Silver Rank Means ✦

Silver Rank marks the transition from novice adventurer to dependable professional. Members are expected to plan ahead, protect less-experienced companions, and complete missions affecting several settlements or an important trade route.

✦ Silver Rank Stages ✦

Silver 1

Character Level 5

  • Reach character level 5 and complete the advancement requirements following the previous major rank.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +5 guild-training bonus.
  • Gain access to Silver contracts and regional assignments.
  • Receive standard lodging at participating Guild halls.

Silver 2

Character Level 6

  • Reach character level 6 and complete the advancement requirements following Silver 1.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +6 guild-training bonus.
  • Receive a 10% Guild merchant discount.
  • Use designated training grounds and general research collections.

Silver 3

Character Level 7

  • Reach character level 7 and complete the advancement requirements following Silver 2.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +7 guild-training bonus.
  • Request basic transportation or common consumables.
  • Receive priority consideration for time-sensitive regional work.

Silver 4

Character Level 8

  • Reach character level 8 and complete the advancement requirements following Silver 3.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +8 guild-training bonus.
  • Supervise approved Bronze FTO assignments.
  • Qualify for Gold 1 after reaching level 9.

Guild Benefit

Skill Training

Each incremental promotion allows the character to train one chosen skill with an appropriate Guild NPC. The training grants an additional bonus on top of the character’s existing skill modifier.

The maximum guild-training bonus equals the character level represented by the current rank stage. Silver 1 permits a +5 bonus, while Silver 4 permits a +8 bonus.

Training the same skill again replaces its previous guild-training bonus with the new higher value; earlier Guild bonuses to that same skill do not stack together.

✦ Benefits and Privileges ✦

Regional Authority

Accept assignments crossing settlement boundaries.

Improved Lodging

Receive standard Guild lodging during active contracts.

Merchant Discount

Receive a 10% discount from participating Guild merchants.

Research Access

Use general archives, maps, and regional threat reports.

Contract Support

Request basic transportation or common consumables.

Training Responsibility

Silver 4 members may supervise approved Bronze FTO assignments.

✦ Typical Silver Contracts ✦

Silver contracts may involve dangerous creatures, organized criminals, remote travel, sensitive negotiations, or investigations that cross local jurisdictions.

Advancement

Characters should normally complete three to five successful quests, earn the required advancement experience or milestone, and reach the minimum character level for the next stage. Incremental promotions may provide currency or recognition, while advancement into a new major rank should include a Rank Advancement Ceremony and a level-appropriate magic item.

Dungeon Master Guidance

Silver quests should expand the party’s responsibilities and geographic reach. Introduce planning, teamwork, and multiple factions.

Guild-training bonuses are intentionally powerful and are added to the character’s existing skill modifier. Treat them as a high-powered homebrew reward. Upgrade rather than repeatedly stack the Guild bonus for the same skill.

The reward table is a campaign guideline. Adjust it for campaign economy, party resources, mission duration, and consequences. All rewards are listed per individual character.
