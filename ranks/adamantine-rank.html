Guild Classification

Adamantine Rank

Character Levels 17–20

Adamantine adventurers are living legends whose actions shape history. They confront threats that endanger nations, worlds, planes, and the continued existence of the Adventurer’s Guild itself.

✦ What Adamantine Rank Means ✦

Adamantine Rank is the Guild’s highest formal classification. Members are trusted with extraordinary authority, access, and resources because few others are capable of completing their assignments.

✦ Adamantine Rank Stages ✦

Adamantine 1

Character Level 17

  • Reach character level 17 and complete the advancement requirements following the previous major rank.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +17 guild-training bonus.
  • Gain access to Adamantine contracts and Guild-wide emergencies.
  • Receive direct access to senior Guild leadership.
Adamantine 2

Character Level 18

  • Reach character level 18 and complete the advancement requirements following Adamantine 1.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +18 guild-training bonus.
  • Receive a 25% Guild merchant discount.
  • Use nearly all Guild archives except personally sealed records.
Adamantine 3

Character Level 19

  • Reach character level 19 and complete the advancement requirements following Adamantine 2.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +19 guild-training bonus.
  • Request extraordinary transport, specialists, and major resources.
  • Exercise temporary command authority during Guild emergencies.
Adamantine 4

Character Level 20

  • Reach character level 20 and complete the advancement requirements following Adamantine 3.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +20 guild-training bonus.
  • Request approved very rare or legendary resources.
  • Receive a unique title as an Adamantine 4 Living Legend.

Guild Benefit

Skill Training

Each incremental promotion allows the character to train one chosen skill with an appropriate Guild NPC. The training grants an additional bonus on top of the character’s existing skill modifier.

The maximum guild-training bonus equals the character level represented by the current rank stage. Adamantine 1 permits a +17 bonus, while Adamantine 4 permits a +20 bonus.

Training the same skill again replaces its previous guild-training bonus with the new higher value; earlier Guild bonuses to that same skill do not stack together.

✦ Benefits and Privileges ✦

Legendary Contracts

Confront threats affecting nations, worlds, or planes.

Guild-Wide Authority

Coordinate emergency operations and major Guild resources.

Merchant Discount

Receive a 25% discount from participating Guild merchants.

Archive Access

Use nearly all Guild records except personally sealed materials.

Extraordinary Support

Request specialist teams, powerful transportation, and major logistics.

Living Legend

Adamantine 4 members receive a unique title and lasting recognition.

✦ Typical Adamantine Contracts ✦

Adamantine contracts may involve world-ending artifacts, ancient powers, planar wars, divine conflicts, or enemies whose defeat permanently changes the campaign setting.

Advancement

Characters should normally complete three to five successful quests, earn the required advancement experience or milestone, and reach the minimum character level for the next stage. Incremental promotions may provide currency or recognition, while advancement into a new major rank should include a Rank Advancement Ceremony and a level-appropriate magic item.

Dungeon Master Guidance

Adamantine quests should resolve major campaign arcs and permanently affect the setting. Reputation, alliances, sacrifices, and long-term consequences should matter greatly.

Guild-training bonuses are intentionally powerful and are added to the character’s existing skill modifier. Treat them as a high-powered homebrew reward. Upgrade rather than repeatedly stack the Guild bonus for the same skill.

The reward table is a campaign guideline. Adjust it for campaign economy, party resources, mission duration, and consequences. All rewards are listed per individual character.
