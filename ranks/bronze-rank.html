Guild Classification

Bronze Rank

Character Levels 1–4

Bronze adventurers are newly accepted members of the Adventurer’s Guild. They learn guild procedures, build a reputation through local contracts, and demonstrate that they can complete assignments safely and reliably.

✦ What Bronze Rank Means ✦

Bronze Rank represents the beginning of a character’s official guild career. Members are expected to follow instructions, communicate clearly, protect the public, and know when to seek help from more experienced adventurers.

✦ Bronze Rank Stages ✦

Bronze 1

Character Level 1

  • Complete three Field Training and Observation quests with a Guild Trainer.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +1 guild-training bonus.
  • Receive an official Bronze badge and permission to accept basic contracts.
  • Use public Guild halls, notice boards, and common meeting rooms.

Bronze 2

Character Level 2

  • Reach character level 2 and complete the advancement requirements following Bronze 1.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +2 guild-training bonus.
  • Receive reduced-cost common lodging on Guild business.
  • Request basic maps and local contract information.

Bronze 3

Character Level 3

  • Reach character level 3 and complete the advancement requirements following Bronze 2.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +3 guild-training bonus.
  • Accept Normal Bronze contracts without supervision.
  • Receive priority mundane equipment repairs and replacement supplies.

Bronze 4

Character Level 4

  • Reach character level 4 and complete the advancement requirements following Bronze 3.
  • Train one chosen skill to gain or upgrade to a +4 guild-training bonus.
  • Accept Hard Bronze contracts and assist with recruit training.
  • Qualify for Silver 1 after reaching level 5.

Guild Benefit

Skill Training

Each incremental promotion allows the character to train one chosen skill with an appropriate Guild NPC. The training grants an additional bonus on top of the character’s existing skill modifier.

The maximum guild-training bonus equals the character level represented by the current rank stage. Bronze 1 permits a +1 bonus, while Bronze 4 permits a +4 bonus.

Training the same skill again replaces its previous guild-training bonus with the new higher value; earlier Guild bonuses to that same skill do not stack together.

✦ Benefits and Privileges ✦

Guild Recognition

Use a Bronze badge as proof of official membership.

Local Contracts

Accept paid local assignments and entry-level Guild work.

Guild Training

Train one chosen skill at each incremental promotion.

Travel Support

Request basic maps, common lodging, and limited mundane supplies.

Reputation Building

Successful contracts improve standing with Guild NPCs.

Recruit Assistance

Bronze 4 members may assist with supervised recruit training.

✦ Typical Bronze Contracts ✦

Bronze contracts usually remain local and place limited communities, travelers, merchants, or property at risk. These quests test judgment and reliability without immediately exposing new members to kingdom-level threats.

Advancement

Characters should normally complete three to five successful quests, earn the required advancement experience or milestone, and reach the minimum character level for the next stage. Incremental promotions may provide currency or recognition, while advancement into a new major rank should include a Rank Advancement Ceremony and a level-appropriate magic item.

Dungeon Master Guidance

Use Bronze quests to teach Guild procedures, reporting expectations, classifications, and the consequences of success or failure.

Guild-training bonuses are intentionally powerful and are added to the character’s existing skill modifier. Treat them as a high-powered homebrew reward. Upgrade rather than repeatedly stack the Guild bonus for the same skill.

The reward table is a campaign guideline. Adjust it for campaign economy, party resources, mission duration, and consequences. All rewards are listed per individual character.
